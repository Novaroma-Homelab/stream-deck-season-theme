Novaroma Season Themes gives your Stream Deck and the Stream Deck Neo infobar the look of the Nova Roma homelab: four seasons, light and dark, one calm colour system. Follow the calendar or pick the season yourself.

Features
- Season and Theme keys: follow the calendar (light by day, dark by night) or lock a season
- Deck wallpaper: one picture across all keys and the infobar that slides to the next season or time of day
- Home Assistant keys: toggle a light or switch and see its state, or call a service
- Launcher and Icon keys with about 100 season-coloured icons
- Study Timer with progress on the infobar
- Infobar for Stream Deck Neo: auto scenes with transitions, page indicators in 6 styles for any number of pages, countdown, timer, banner, server status
- English and German, following the app language

Requirements
Stream Deck 7.6 or newer. Infobar actions need a Stream Deck Neo. Home Assistant keys need your address and a long-lived access token.

Privacy
No analytics, no accounts. The plugin only contacts the addresses you enter. Your token stays in the Stream Deck settings. The current season is written to .novaroma/state.json in your home folder.
