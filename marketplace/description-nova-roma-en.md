Novaroma Season Themes gives your Stream Deck and the Stream Deck Neo infobar the look of the Nova Roma homelab: four seasons, light and dark, one calm colour system. Follow the calendar or pick the season yourself.

Born in a homelab called Nova Roma, for a desk where keys, infobar and smart home belong together.

Features
- Season and Theme keys: follow the calendar (light by day, dark by night) or lock a season
- Home Assistant keys: toggle a light or switch and see its state, or call any service, scene or script in the background
- Launcher and Icon keys with about 100 icons in the season colours
- Study Timer with progress on the infobar
- Infobar for Stream Deck Neo: page indicators and clock in 6 styles, icon, text, banner, countdown, season progress, server status, backdrop
- English and German, following the app language

Requirements
Stream Deck 7.6 or newer. Infobar actions need a Stream Deck Neo. Home Assistant keys need your address and a long-lived access token.

Privacy
No analytics and no accounts. The plugin only contacts the addresses you enter. Your token stays in the Stream Deck app settings. The current season is written to .novaroma/state.json in your home folder.
