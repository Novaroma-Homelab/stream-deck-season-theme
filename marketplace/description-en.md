Novaroma Season Themes gives your Stream Deck keys and the Stream Deck Neo infobar a seasonal look. Follow the calendar or set a season yourself, switch between light and dark, control Home Assistant and add launcher keys, a study timer and infobar styles.

Features
- Season and Theme keys: cycle, set a fixed value or follow the calendar (light by day, dark by night)
- Home Assistant keys: toggle a light or switch and see its state, or call any service, scene or script, all in the background
- Launcher and Icon keys with about 100 icons in the season colours
- Study Timer with progress on the infobar
- Infobar actions for Stream Deck Neo: pages and clock (6 styles), page name, icon and text, clock, season progress, countdown, server status, backdrop, banner
- English and German, following the app language

Requirements
Stream Deck 7.6 or newer. Infobar actions need a Stream Deck Neo. Home Assistant keys need your address and a long-lived access token.

Privacy
No analytics and no accounts. The plugin only contacts the addresses you enter. Your token stays in the Stream Deck app settings. The current season and theme are written to .novaroma/state.json in your home folder for your own scripts.
