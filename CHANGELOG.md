# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Own setting "Infobar background" (follow the deck look, plain, arches, glow, lines, dots, picture). The infobar is not covered by the app background image, so "No background" gives it the season colour.
- Deck look with four modes: tiles, no background, background, animated background. New field Background style. The old Deck background setting is still read.
- Animated background: slow stepped motion (snow, leaves, petals, fireflies, twinkling stars), at most about 6 image updates per second.
- Deck background "Transparent": keys draw only the symbol, so the Stream Deck app's own background image shows through.
- Optional fixed icon colour (season and light/dark) in the global settings; the background and infobar keep following the automatic season.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Season and theme keys that follow the meteorological season and light or dark by time of day, or a fixed choice.
- Neo infobar actions: page indicators (six styles), clock, season, text, banner, countdown, timer, homelab status, icon and backdrop.
- Launcher key for links and apps, study timer, icon key from the Season Icons pack.
- Home Assistant keys: toggle and service call, with state shown on the key.
- English and German interface.
- Deck wallpaper: one picture (aqueduct scene with sun or moon, or soft waves) across all keys and the infobar, sliding, fading or rising to the next season or time of day.
- Infobar auto scenes: clock, season, moon and year, countdown, study timer and a Home Assistant value, switching by themselves with a transition.
- Deck background styles for all keys and infobars: arches, glow, fine lines, dots.
- Page indicators work with any number of pages (up to 24): icons shrink and finally turn into dots, extra page icons can be typed in as names.
